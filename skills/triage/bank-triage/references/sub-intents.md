# Sub-intent labels

Use the closest label below. If none fits, write a short snake_case label of
your own.

## fraud_security
| Label | Typical wording |
|---|---|
| unauthorized_charge | "a charge I never made", "don't recognise this transaction" |
| suspected_account_takeover | unknown device or login, password changed without the customer, locked out after strange activity |
| lost_card | "left my card", "can't find my card" |
| stolen_card | "my wallet was stolen", "card taken" |
| phishing_reported | the customer clicked a link, shared a code, or received a fake bank call or text |
| scam_payment | the customer was tricked into sending money themselves |

## account_services
| Label | Typical wording |
|---|---|
| address_change | moved house, new mailing address |
| statement_request | needs a statement or proof of balance |
| pin_reset | forgot or wants to change their PIN |
| fee_dispute | overdraft, maintenance, or ATM fee they think is wrong |
| account_access_issue | can't log in, with no sign of fraud |

## lending_mortgages
| Label | Typical wording |
|---|---|
| loan_application | applying for, or checking the status of, a personal or auto loan |
| mortgage_rate_inquiry | current mortgage rates, shopping around |
| payoff_statement | payoff quote for a sale or refinance |
| refinance_inquiry | wants to refinance |
| loan_payment_issue | a missed, late, or wrong loan payment |

## general_inquiry
| Label | Typical wording |
|---|---|
| branch_hours | opening times |
| branch_location | where the nearest branch or ATM is |
| savings_rate | APY or interest on savings or CDs |
| product_information | what accounts or cards the bank offers |
